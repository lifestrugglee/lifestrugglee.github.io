About

I work on getting reliable, reusable information out of electronic health records with methods that respect patient privacy: synthetic clinical text, small language models that can run locally, clinician-guided annotation, and evaluation across institutions.

Most of my Ph.D. work, in Dr. Xiaoqian Jiang's lab at UTHealth Houston, applies these ideas to dental records. In 2025 I spent a summer at PAII Inc. fine-tuning and serving a 32B vision-language model for medical images and clinical conversations.

Before informatics I trained as a respiratory therapist in Taiwan and then worked as a software engineer. Clinical training taught me what a note is for; engineering taught me to build the pipelines and tools that make a model useful outside a notebook.

I'm looking for research scientist and applied ML roles in clinical AI and language models, starting January 2027.

Selected work

  • Self-prompting small language models for clinical extraction

    Preprint, arXiv 2026, under review

    Small language models write and refine their own entity-specific prompts, then learn from 200 annotated notes through QLoRA fine-tuning and direct preference optimization. The models are small enough to run locally, so notes never go to an outside API.

    Read the preprint
    0.864micro-F1 across 19 entity types on a 1,000-note held-out set

  • Synthetic notes that transfer across institutions

    JAMIA Open, 2025

    Trained a periodontal diagnosis extractor with spaCy and RoBERTa on 450 GPT-4-generated synthetic notes, then evaluated it on 720 expert-annotated notes from two institutions. It kept 99% of its in-domain performance at the second site.

    Read the paper
    0.95weighted F1 across five diagnostic entities

  • Privacy risks in LLM-generated clinical notes

    JAMIA, 2025

    Designed PHI-injection and leakage-analysis experiments across 9,817 LLM-generated notes with downstream ICD-9 coding. Injecting synthetic PHI beat standard de-identification on downstream accuracy in 6 of 9 configurations.

    Read the paper
    >99%less PHI exposure with keyword conditioning

  • Struggle Annotator

    Open-source annotation tool, Python and Streamlit

    A clinician-facing app with real-time span editing, so reviewers correct model pre-annotations directly instead of labeling from scratch. I built and deployed it and trained our clinical annotators on the guidelines and the tool.

    View the codeTry the demo
    ~50%less annotation time, estimated

  • Medical vision-language model, fine-tuned and served

    Research Scientist Intern, PAII Inc., 2025

    Fine-tuned a 32B vision-language model on medical image-dialogue pairs with LoRA, improving diagnosis extraction over the zero-shot baseline. Quantized it to 4-bit with GPTQ, served it with vLLM across four A100s using tensor parallelism, and grounded prompts in UMLS concepts.

    32Bparameters, served on 4 A100 GPUs

Also in progress
